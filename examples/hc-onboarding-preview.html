A Century of Leadership

Since 1916, Hilscher-Clarke Electric Company has set the standard in electrical contracting. What started as State Electric has grown into a multi-state operation serving commercial, industrial, specialty, and residential customers.

1916
State Electric becomes a member of the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA).
1926
Walt Hilscher and Roy Clarke purchase State Electric, renaming it Hilscher-Clarke Electric Company.
1945
Hilscher-Clarke Electric Company is incorporated.
2020s
Expansion into Columbus, Florida, and launch of HC Energy, HC Forestry, and HC Manufacturing divisions.
Key Fact
Today, Hilscher-Clarke serves 17 core markets across 4 business categories, with offices throughout Ohio, Pennsylvania, West Virginia, and Florida.

Safety Is Non-Negotiable

In the electrical industry, safety isn't a policy — it's a way of life. Every person on every jobsite is responsible for maintaining the highest standards of safety for themselves and their team.

Stop Work Authority
Every employee has the right — and the obligation — to stop work if they see an unsafe condition. No exceptions, no retaliation.
PPE Requirements
Hard hats, safety glasses, arc-rated clothing, gloves, and appropriate footwear are required on every jobsite.
Daily Briefings
Every crew starts with a safety briefing covering the day's hazards, tasks, and emergency procedures.
Incident Reporting
All incidents, near-misses, and unsafe conditions must be reported immediately. Early reporting prevents future injuries.
Safety Culture
Our Corporate Safety Director and safety teams maintain incident rates well below the national average. Your awareness and participation keep that record strong.
